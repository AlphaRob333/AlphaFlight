Preflight Risk Assessment

The school's PAVE matrix: rate each item 1 to 5 in an honest, self-evaluating way, then total the column. 25–49 is normal, 50–74 elevated, 75 and above high. Any 5 is a No-Go. In the elevated or high range solo flight is not authorized; on a dual flight the CFI rates the same items to transfer some of the risk.
